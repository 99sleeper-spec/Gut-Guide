Search settings

Product search uses the USDA's free database of US packaged foods. The shared demo key allows about 50 searches a day. A free personal key raises that to 1,000 an hour: sign up at fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-key-signup, then paste the key from the email here. It stays on this phone only.

Eating out

Best move: most chains post a full ingredient list online. Open it, copy the ingredients for the dish, and paste them into Check → Scan or paste for a red, yellow or green answer.

Restaurants rarely list every ingredient, and most sauces, marinades and seasoning blends contain garlic or onion. On gentle FODMAP, small amounts are often tolerated. These are starting points, not guarantees.

Chain ingredient lists

What to ask for

Sauce and dressing on the side, or oil and vinegar with lemon.
Protein grilled plain, with salt, pepper and oil only.
Swap beans, onion rings or slaw for rice, a plain potato or steamed vegetables.
"No onion or garlic" works for fresh-made dishes, but not for pre-marinated meat or ready-made sauces.
Real maple syrup instead of pancake syrup, which is usually high fructose corn syrup.
